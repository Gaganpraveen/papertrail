## Why this paper matters

The authors propose that the electron mass can be derived from space-time geometry within Deformed Special Relativity (DSR) by utilizing experimental knowledge of local Lorentz invariance breakdown and a Minkowskian threshold energy. ## Problem

The origin of particle mass remains an open theoretical problem, as the Standard Model cannot explain why particles bear specific masses, and the electron's mass origin is still mysterious despite the successes of the Dirac equation.
