The problem

The origin of particle mass remains an open theoretical problem, as the Standard Model cannot explain why particles bear specific masses, and the electron's mass origin is still mysterious despite the successes of the Dirac equation.

It is shown that the value of the electron mass can be derived from space-time geometry via the experimental knowledge of the LLI breakdown parameter and the Minkowskian threshold energy, suggesting that mass generation may be related to the dependence of mass on the metric background.
